# פרומפט ל-GPT: ה"לוגו" הקבוע של כרטיסי התצוגה

> נכתב 2026-10-22. התמונה שתתקבל תשובץ בצד השמאלי של כל כרטיס (מקום של 440x500).
> הכותרת והמשפט נשארים בצד הימני, טקסט אמיתי. לכן בתמונה עצמה אסור שיהיה טקסט.

```
Create an illustration to be used as a fixed visual emblem on link-preview cards for a series of step-by-step software tutorials (screen-by-screen guides for teachers and school staff: Word, OneDrive, Forms, Outlook, Copilot).

Concept: "we show you exactly where to click". A clean, friendly app window or screen, with one element on it highlighted by a rounded frame in the warm accent colour, a small circle in the same accent with the number 1 next to it, and a mouse cursor pointing at it. Optionally a soft hint of a path or steps leading forward. It should feel calm, clear and reassuring, not busy.

Style:
- Soft 3D / layered illustration with real depth: gentle shadows, slightly raised layers, subtle gradients. Not flat, not clip-art, not a generic stock icon.
- Clean and professional, with a warm and human feel. Minimal detail: 3–5 main shapes.
- Rounded corners everywhere.

Colors (you choose, in harmony with the guides):
- The guides themselves use a deep teal (#0f7c80, #0e4f55) as the main colour, warm orange (#ee6a3c) for highlights and click marks, white and pale teal surfaces, and sometimes a burgundy accent (#8c2f4a). These are a reference, not a fixed palette.
- Choose a palette that feels like it belongs to the same family as the guides, so the image and the guides look related. You may add tones, shift shades or introduce a complementary colour, as long as the whole stays calm, light and cohesive.
- Keep one warm accent for the highlighted element, so the eye goes straight to "where to click".
- Use a light, calm, plain background, edge to edge (no frame or border), so it can sit on a light card.
- Avoid bright red and lemon yellow.
- Tell me the main colours you used (hex codes), so I can match the card around the image.

Strict rules:
- NO text, letters, words or logos anywhere in the image. The number "1" in the small circle is the only exception.
- Portrait format, 4:5 ratio (for example 1024x1280), with the subject centered and comfortable margins on all sides.
- It must stay readable when shown small (about 200px wide).

Please give me 3 different variations of this concept.
```

## אחרי שיש תמונה
שומרים אותה (PNG) ושולחים לנתנאל. הוא ישבץ אותה בכלי `_build/preview_cards.py` במקום הצילומים, ויציג את כל הכרטיסים לאישור לפני פרסום.
